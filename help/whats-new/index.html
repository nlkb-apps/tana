What's new

2026-10-07: A new look, and quicker ways to add games

The front page now shows what Tana is in one go: a sample shelf, a link to see a real one, and the search box right there. When you're signed in, it opens with your own newest boxes and shortcuts to scan, tick off a platform or import a list.

You can now add a game from search with one tap on the circle next to it, and game pages have Add to collection, Want it and Share at the top. Picking an edition is a single tap from a list of every edition. Your collection page shows your numbers at a glance, has an Add games button with every way to add in one place, and can sort your shelf by newest first. On a computer, the menu moves to the top bar and pages use the wider screen.

2026-10-06: Game-Key Cards for new Western releases

New Western-only Switch 2 games are now marked as either a full game card or a Game-Key Card when they're added, the same way Japanese releases already were.

2026-10-05: New games from everywhere, and Recently added

Tana now looks for new Western releases every week as well as Japanese ones, and games that were announced before they came out are now added once they're released. The home page shows the games most recently added to Tana, from every region, newest first.

2026-09-29: Help pages

These help pages are now live at tana.gamingjapanese.com/help/, with a link at the top of every public page and at the bottom of the app.

2026-09-28: Discord server and subreddit

Tana now has a Discord server (discord.gg/RfwNdPS8G) and a subreddit, r/tanashelf. You'll find links on the Community page and at the bottom of every page.

2026-09-27: Cover variants and edition notes

You can now add a Cover variant when a game was printed with different box art under the same serial, and describe what makes an edition different. Comments on game pages also count toward your contribution orb now.

2026-09-25: Sharing, Community and the leaderboard

Your collection now has its own share link with a picture of your shelf, a shelf image you can download, and embed code. There's a new Community tab with the leaderboard, a Following feed and wants matches, and game pages show how many collectors own and want each edition.

2026-09-25: Asia and Korea

Asian and Korean releases now have their own regions, in ochre and plum.

2026-09-25: More editions

Serials and European country editions for PS1, PS2, Saturn, Dreamcast and GameCube, and reprint lines like Greatest Hits, Platinum and Player's Choice across most platforms. Japanese edition names now show in Japanese.

2026-09-24: The shelf view

Collections now open as a shelf, with real box sizes. The list view is still there.

2026-09-24: Western-only games

About 12,000 games that only came out outside Japan were added, along with the Western only and Physical in the West only stamps and filters.
