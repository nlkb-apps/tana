Help, Wants

Wants matches

Community > Wants matches shows two lists:

  • Collectors who want games you own
  • Collectors who own games you want

Only public collections are shown by name. Private collections are still counted, so you might see something like "2 collectors want this" with no names.

Tana doesn't handle sales or trades. If you want to get in touch with someone, do it outside Tana, i.e. on our Discord or subreddit.
