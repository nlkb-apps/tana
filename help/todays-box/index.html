Today's Box

Today's Box is a daily game: guess a game from its box. There's one box a day for everyone, and a new one opens at midnight Japan time. Play at tana.gamingjapanese.com/box/, or tap Today's Box on the home page. You don't need an account to play.

Playing

The box starts as an 8-bit mosaic, 8 blocks across, too coarse to read any title. You also see the platform. Type a game and pick it from the list. You can type its English name as sold in the West, its Japanese title, or the romanised Japanese title, and numbers work either way (Street Fighter 2 finds Street Fighter II). Each game appears once, with every platform it came out on, and any of them counts: you're guessing the game, not the platform. Where a game was renamed for the West, the list shows its Japanese name too, so the US Final Fantasy III says "In Japan: Final Fantasy VI". Use the arrow keys and Enter, or tap. Each wrong guess, or each tap of Skip and sharpen, makes the box sharper and adds a clue: the year, then the publisher, the genre and the first letter of its Western title. You have six tries. When you solve it, or use all six, you see the whole box, the game, and a line on how it came out in Japan and the West.

Any version of the right game counts. If today's box is the Japanese Super Famicom release, picking the game by its English name is right too.

Stamps, streaks and plates

Each day you finish today's box earns a stamp on the month's stamp card, like the stamp cards Japanese children collect for summer morning exercises. Days in a row make your streak. If you stamp every box in a month, that month's brass plate goes under your shelf for good, and visitors to your shelf see it too.

Boxes from earlier days can still be played from their link, but they don't earn stamps or count toward your streak.

Without an account

You can play every day without an account. Your stamps and streak are kept on the device you play on. When you create a free account or sign in, today's and yesterday's results move into your account, and from then on your stamps, streak and plates follow you to any device.

Sharing

When you finish, you get a result to post, like this:

  • Tana · Today's Box #12 3/6
  • 🟥🟥🟩⬛⬛⬛ 🔥5

It shows how many tries you needed and your streak, without giving the game away. Post on X opens a post with it ready, or tap Copy.

If you follow other collectors, their results for the day show under yours, again without the answer.
