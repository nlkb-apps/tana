Help, Finding games

Filters

Sort options are best match (what the system deems as most relevant— recommended in most cases), title (あいうえお) which presents results in the Japanese alphabetical order, newest releases showing newest games first going back, oldest release doing the opposite (showing the oldest titles first), and recently updated which shows the most recently modified titles on Tana first.

There are also Japan-only and western-only releases to be sorted. As it happens, most major games are released across both Japan and international audiences, however there exists a substantial library of titles that never left Japan and vice versa. Use these filters to find those.

More filters include further options to slice and dice your search results. You can decide genre (rpg, fps, etc.), decade (90s, 2000s, etc), publisher (Nintendo, Sega, etc.), region (Japan only, came west, western only, physical in the west only), and media. The counts shown next to each filter show the total number of games for each query available.

Using clear filters/search all platforms lets you search through the entire library across Tana.
