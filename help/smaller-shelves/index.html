Help, Your collection

Smaller shelves

Your main shelf holds everything you own. On top of that you can make up to five smaller shelves, each with games you pick out yourself: your favourites, what you're playing right now, your grails, a shelf for one series. Each one has its own name, its own style and its own order.

Your shelves are in a row above the shelf on your collection page, each drawn as a little case with its first few boxes. Tap one to open it, and tap Main shelf to go back.

Making a smaller shelf

Tap New shelf at the end of the row and give it a name (there are a few ideas under the box if you want one). Start with lets you fill it straight away with your Japan-only games, your sealed copies, your complete-in-box copies or everything on one platform. Or leave it empty and choose the games yourself.

Choosing the games

Open the shelf and tap Choose games. You get a list of everything in your collection: tick what belongs on this shelf and untick what doesn't. You can search the list or narrow it to one platform, and Tick all shown and Untick all shown work on whatever the list is showing.

A game on a smaller shelf is still on your main shelf. It's the same copy, just shown in two places, so editing or removing it changes it everywhere. A copy can be on more than one smaller shelf.

In list view you can also tap Shelves on any copy to put it on, or take it off, your smaller shelves.

Order and style

A smaller shelf works like the main one. The Order menu, Arrange and Style are all there, and whatever you choose only changes this shelf. A new shelf keeps the games in the order you added them until you change it. If you don't pick a style, it takes the style of your main shelf.

A smaller shelf is only as wide as it needs to be, so a shelf of five games looks like a small case of five games.

Renaming, moving and deleting

Rename, move or delete is next to Choose games. Move earlier and Move later change where the shelf sits in the row. Deleting a shelf doesn't remove any games: they stay in your collection and on your main shelf.

Who sees them

If your collection is public, visitors see your smaller shelves in the same row, in your styles. Empty ones are hidden from visitors. Your share page shows them under your main shelf from the next nightly update, and you can share a link that opens a single smaller shelf (see Sharing your shelf).
