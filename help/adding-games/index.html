Adding a game to your collection

To add a game, open its page, find the edition that matches your copy, and add it to your collection. If you're not sure which edition you have, check the serial on the spine or the disc and compare it with the ones listed.

You can then fill in the details for your copy:

  • Condition: Sealed, Mint, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Junk (ジャンク)
  • What's included (box, manual, cartridge or disc, obi, etc.)
  • Notes, the price you paid, and your own estimate of its value

Notes and prices are private. Nobody else can see them, even if your collection is public.

If you own more than one copy of the same edition, add each one separately. Each copy has its own condition and photos.
