Help, Getting started

Finding your way around

On a phone, you will see the search, collection, wants, community, and account icons at the bottom of the screen. On a computer or a wide tablet they sit in the bar at the top instead, next to the Tana logo, and your account button is at the top right.

  • Search brings you to the search function to let you find what is across the Tana database.
  • Collection displays your own collection of games you can log and grow on Tana.
  • Wants is a growing list you can manage of the games and copies of games you’re looking for. Tap want next to a game and it will show up here.
  • Community tab brings you to all the features to interact with others including leaderboards and matching wants.
  • Account is where you can manage your bio, display name, dark/light setting, etc.

When you're signed in, the home page opens with your own shelf: the boxes you added most recently, how many copies you have, and four shortcuts (scan a barcode, tick off a platform, import a list, and your wantlist). If your shelf is still empty, you'll see every console instead, so you can pick one and start ticking.

Below that is the search box, and then Browse by platform, a row of spines for every platform on Tana with the number of games on each. Tap one to see its whole library.

When the search box is empty, the home page shows the games most recently added to Tana, newest first. Use the platform menu or filters if you only want to see some of them.

There are also public game pages you can use to share outside of the community at Tana.gamingjapanese.com/game/<gamename>, but a url with a # in it is part of the app itself.
