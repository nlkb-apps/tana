Help, Your collection

Shelf view and list view

Your collection opens on the shelf view by default. Each game is drawn as a box on a wooden shelf, in its platform's color, with the title printed on the spine. Box sizes follow the real cases, so a PS2 or GameCube game stands tall and thin, a PS1 jewel case is short, and a Game Boy box is small.

Hover over a box (or tap it once on a phone) to see the title, platform, region, edition and serial. Tap it again to open the game page.

Shelves hold up to 20 games on a phone and more on a wider screen, and re-arrange to fit, with a small gap between platforms like a bookend. The filter box above the shelf works here too, and the shelf re-flows as you type.

Next to the filter you can sort the shelf by platform (the default), newest first (the copies you added most recently), or by title. Tana remembers the sort on each device.

At the top of your collection you'll see your numbers at a glance: games, platforms, how many are Japan-only, and how many are complete in box.

If you'd rather see a plain list, use the Shelf | List toggle at the top right. Tana remembers your choice on each device.
