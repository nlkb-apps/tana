Help, Sharing and community

Your public shelf and privacy

Your collection can be public or private. You can change this in Account with Anyone can see my collection and wantlist.

A public collection can be shared, followed, and shows up by name in wants matches and on the leaderboard. A private one is only visible to you. If you make a public collection private, its share page is taken down at the next nightly update.

Either way, your notes and prices are always private, and adult titles never show up on share pages.
