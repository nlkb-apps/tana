Platforms covered

Tana covers 62 platforms, from the Famicom to the Switch 2. That includes Japan's own computers like the MSX, PC-88, PC-98, X68000, and FM Towns, which most other databases skip.

PC games (DOS and Windows), hardware, and accessories aren't covered yet.
