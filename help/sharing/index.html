Help, Sharing and community

Sharing your shelf

Once your collection is public, you can share it from the Share panel on your collection page. You'll find:

  • Your link, ready to copy: tana.gamingjapanese.com/u/<username>/
  • Buttons for X, Bluesky, Reddit and Facebook, plus your phone's own share sheet
  • Download shelf image, which draws a picture of your shelf in portrait (good for Instagram)
  • Embed code, to put a small shelf on a blog or a forum post

Your link works right away. The preview picture that shows up on social sites is a drawing of your actual shelf, and it refreshes overnight.

Share pages are kept out of search engines, so they're only seen by the people you share them with.
