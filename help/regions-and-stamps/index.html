Regions and stamps

Each game has a region version attached to it-- this is what makes Tana special in the game collecting database market. Tana is the most specialized when it comes to regionalizing your collection.

-- Vermillion - Japan release. -- Indigo - North America -- Pine-green - Europe -- Ochre - Asia -- Plum-purple - Korea

Japan only and Western only are two categories of games that indicate whether a release was made exclusively in one region or not. Physical in the west only is another specific categorization where physical games may exist only in western markets but not in Japan. Asian and Korean releases also exist but do not count as "went west".
