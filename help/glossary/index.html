Glossary

  • Game: the title itself, across all regions and platforms
  • Release: a game's version in one region on one platform
  • Edition: a specific product within a release (standard, reprint, limited, cover variant, etc.). This is what you add to your collection.
  • Copy: one physical copy you own of an edition
  • Main shelf: everything in your collection
  • Box: one of nine numbered lists of any games from the catalogue, owned or not, under Wants & lists
  • Today's Box (今日の一箱): the daily game of guessing a game from its box, shown as a mosaic that sharpens with each guess
  • Plate: a brass plate under your shelf for stamping every Today's Box in a month
  • Census: the running count of every public My 9: most picked games, keystones, platforms and eras
  • Keystone: the game in the middle cubby of a My 9
  • My 9 (私を構成する9本): a 3 by 3 grid of the nine games that define you, kept in one of your boxes
  • Box art: the cover shown for a release, from scans, Wikipedia or members' photos, always with the release's region patch
  • Region patch: the small JP, NA or EU label on a box, showing the region of the release
  • Smaller shelf: one of up to five shelves of games you pick from your collection, with its own name, order and style
  • Serial / product code: the code on the spine, disc or cartridge, i.e. SLPS-00700
  • JAN: the Japanese barcode number on the box
  • Obi: the paper spine card that comes with many Japanese games
  • CIB: complete in box
  • Loose: the cartridge or disc on its own
  • Reprint line: a budget re-release series, i.e. PlayStation the Best or Greatest Hits
  • Cover variant: a printing with different box art under the same serial
  • Country edition: a European version for one country, with its own serial
  • Japan only: never released in North America or Europe
  • Western only: never released in Japan
  • Physical in the West only: Japan only got a download, the West got a physical release
  • Verified: a copy with an approved photo of that copy
  • Wanted poster: a gap in the catalogue (no box art, no product code, no photo) that members can fill, with credit on the release
  • Box Battle: the daily vote between a game's Japanese box and its Western box
  • Hall of Boxes: the Box Battle winners that won most clearly
  • Shelf twin: a member whose shelf shares many exact copies with yours
  • Lone keeper: the only member on Tana with a particular edition
  • Circle: a group of members collecting one set of games together
