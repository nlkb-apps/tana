Help, Sharing and community

The weekly letter

The weekly letter is a short email from Nico, who runs Tana. It's written by hand each week, about what came in, what's new on Tana and anything worth talking about.

Under the letter there are two short lists made from Tana itself:

  • **This week on Tana.** New members, the week's Box Battle results, the hardest Today's Box, Wanted posters filled, the circles that grew.
  • **For you.** Lines from your own shelf only: Wanted posters for copies you own, what your shelf twin added, how your Box Battle votes went, games on your wantlist that other members have.

Getting it, or not

Nobody gets the letter unless they say yes. You can say yes when you sign up, from the card that appears on your collection page, or in Account under The weekly letter. Every letter has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, and you can switch it off in Account any time.

Replies go to Nico. The letter is sent through Resend, an email delivery service; see Privacy and your data.
