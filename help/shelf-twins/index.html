Help, Sharing and community

Shelf twins and lone keepers

Tana records the exact copy you own, region and edition included. That lets it find the members who collect like you, and the copies on your shelf that nobody else on Tana has. Open it from Community, then Shelf twins.

Shelf twins

Your shelf twins are the members whose shelves share the most exact copies with yours. A Japanese Super Famicom copy and a North American SNES copy of the same game don't count as the same copy; the same edition of the same release does. Each twin shows how many copies you have in common, which platforms they're mostly on, and how alike your shelves are overall.

From a twin you can follow them, open their shelf, and see how many games from your wantlist they own (see Wants matches).

Only public shelves are compared, and only public members are shown as twins.

Lone keepers

Under "Only on your shelf" you'll find your rarest copies. "Lone keeper" means no other member has logged the same edition. "1 of 3" means three members have it, you included. The list puts Japan-only and better-known games first.

Counts include private collections, but never name anyone.

On your shelf

Your shelf, and every public shelf, shows the closest twin and the number of lone-keeper copies underneath it. Tap either one for the full list.
