# Account settings

Everything about your account lives on the Account page:

- Display name and bio (your username is your public ID and address)
- Anyone can see my collection and wantlist (public or private)
- Hide my photos on my public shelf
- Your usual edition: the edition that goes on your shelf when you tap the circle in search (Japanese, North American, European, Korean or Asian). Saved on each device.
- Appearance: match your device, light, or dark
- Prices: show or hide the Value tab and price information
- Adult titles (see the next page)

You can also delete your account from here.
