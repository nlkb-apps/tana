# The contribution orb

The little orb next to your name shows how much you've added to Tana. Everyone starts with one, and it grows as you contribute.

These count:

- Approved corrections
- Approved regional differences
- Editions you've added an approved photo to
- Comments on game pages (one per game, as long as it says something)

The orb changes color as your count goes up. We'll leave the rest for you to find out.
