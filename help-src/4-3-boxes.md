# Boxes

Next to your wantlist you have nine boxes, numbered 1 to 9 like the boxes in a Pokémon PC. A box holds any games from the catalogue, whether you own them or not, for whatever reason you like: games to play next, games you finished this year, a series you're following, what to look for on your next trip to Akihabara, gift ideas.

You'll find them under Wants & lists. The board at the top shows the wantlist and all nine boxes, each drawn as a little case with its first few games. On a phone the boxes are a 3 by 3 grid.

## Making a box

Tap an empty box. It's called Box 3 (or whichever number it is) until you give it a name, and there are a few ideas under the name box. You can add a short note to say what it's for, and you can start it with everything on your wantlist if you like.

## Putting games in a box

There are two ways:

- In the box, tap Add games and search the whole catalogue. Under each game you'll see its releases (i.e. SFC · JP, SNES · NA). Tap the ones you mean, then Done. Tap one again to take it back out.
- On any game page, tap Add to a box. Pick the release, tick the boxes it should go in, and Save. If you have empty boxes left, you can make a new one with the game in it right there.

A box keeps releases, not your copies, so it doesn't matter whether you own the game. If you do, its spine has a small green tick.

To take a game out, switch the box to the list and tap Take out.

## Order, style and moving around

Each box has its own order (your own order, console, release date, title, series or region), and you can drag games around with Arrange, the same as on your shelf. Style gives the box its own wood, metal, lacquer or paint. A box with no style chosen takes the style of your main shelf.

The arrows on either side of the box's name step to the previous and next box, so you can flick through them like a stack of boxes.

## Renaming, moving and deleting

Rename, move or delete lets you change the name and the note, or move the box to another empty number. Deleting a box only deletes the list. Nothing changes in your collection or on your wantlist.

## Who sees them

If your collection is public, visitors see your wantlist and the boxes with games in them under the Wants & lists tab on your profile. Empty boxes are hidden from visitors.
