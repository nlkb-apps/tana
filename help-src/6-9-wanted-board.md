# The Wanted board

The Wanted board turns the gaps in the catalogue into posters, and lets the members who own those copies fill them. Open it from Community, then Wanted.

## What gets a poster

There are three kinds of poster:

- **Box art.** The release has no box art, or only another region's box is standing in for it.
- **Product code.** An edition someone owns has no product code recorded.
- **First photo.** Nobody has photographed an edition that a member owns.

Posters go up for releases that members own or want, and for the best-known games in the catalogue that still have no box art. The board refreshes every night: copies you add today get their posters tomorrow, and a gap that closes some other way (Tana finding the box art itself, for example) comes off the board.

## Owners first

If you're signed in, the top of the board shows the posters for copies on your shelf under "On your shelf". You have the copy in your hands, so you can fill these today. The rest of the board is ranked by how many members own or want each copy, with Japan-only and well-known games first.

## Filling a poster

- **Box art or first photo.** Choose I'll shoot it and upload a photo you took of your own copy. For box art, choose "Front" as what the photo shows. Once an editor approves it, the poster is filled, and the front photo becomes the release's box art.
- **Product code.** Choose Add the code and type it exactly as it's printed on the spine, the back of the box or the cartridge label. An editor checks it, and once it's saved on the edition, the poster is filled.

The usual photo rules apply: only photos you took, of items you own. See [Adding photos of your copies](5-1-adding-photos.md).

## Credit for good

A filled poster stays on the release card on the game page: "Box art from a photo by …" or "Product code added by …". It counts toward your contributions like any approved photo or correction, and the side of the board shows who filled the most posters this week. If your collection is private, your credit shows as "A member".

## On game pages

A release with an open poster shows a small Wanted note on its card, with a button to fill it if you own that copy.
