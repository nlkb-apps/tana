# Comments and discussion

Every game page has a Discussion section at the bottom where you can leave a comment. It's a good place for notes other collectors would want to know about, i.e. a cover that changed between printings, a hard-to-spot reprint, or what came in the box.

Comments count toward your contribution orb, one per game, as long as they're more than a few words.

For longer conversations, come say hi on our Discord (discord.gg/RfwNdPS8G) or on r/tanashelf.
