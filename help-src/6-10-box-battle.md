# Box Battle

Box Battle puts one game's Japanese box against its Western box, once a day. Pick the box you think is better, then see how everyone voted. Open it from the home page or from Community, then Box Battle. You don't need an account to vote.

## How it works

Each day at midnight Japan time, a new battle opens: the same game, two boxes, side by side, with what's printed on each (title, publisher, year and product code). Tap the box you prefer. Your vote is final, and only after you vote do you see the split.

Tana only draws pairs whose art really differs. Every candidate pair is compared picture by picture first, so you'll never be asked to choose between two copies of the same artwork.

## The score

When a day closes, the box with more votes wins it for its side. The wins add up:

- **All-time score.** Japan against the West, over every battle so far.
- **The monthly cup.** Each month has a theme, such as Famicom vs NES in October and Super Famicom vs SNES in November, and the battles that month come from it.
- **The Hall of Boxes.** The boxes that won most clearly, once a battle has three or more votes.

## Streaks and sharing

Signed-in members build a streak by voting on consecutive days. After you vote, you can post your pick to X or copy it, without giving away anything you shouldn't.

## Without an account

Your vote is kept on the device you vote from. If you create a free account or sign in the same day, your vote moves into your account and starts your streak.
