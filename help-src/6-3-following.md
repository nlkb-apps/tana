# Following collectors

You can follow any collector with a public collection. Open their shelf and tap Follow. Their shelf also shows how many followers they have and how many verified games they own.

Community > Following shows what the people you follow have added recently, newest first.

You can only follow public collections, and if someone makes their collection private they drop out of your feed.
