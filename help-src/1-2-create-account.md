# Creating an account

Create an account to set up your own shelf and contribute to the knowledge base.

Like any online community, you will create a username and password. Your username will be your public-facing ID as well, so choose wisely.

Use an email to confirm your account and go from there.

You can also add a display name and bio on your account page.
