# Creating an account

Create an account to set up your own shelf and contribute to the knowledge base.

Like any online community, you will create a username and password. Your username will be your public-facing ID as well, so choose wisely.

Use an email to confirm your account and go from there. The confirmation email can take a minute and sometimes lands in spam or promotions. If it doesn't come, choose Send it again on the confirmation page, or try signing in and Tana will offer to send it again.

When you sign up you can also tick the box for the weekly letter (see [The weekly letter](6-13-weekly-letter.md)).

Your first sign-in takes you to Welcome, which gets your first games onto your shelf: tick off a whole console, import a list you already keep, or search and scan barcodes. Until your shelf has a game, a thin bar at the top of each page brings you back there.

You can also add a display name and bio on your account page.
