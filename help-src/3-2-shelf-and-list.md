# Shelf view and list view

Your collection opens on the shelf view by default. Each game is drawn as a box on a wooden shelf, in its platform's color, with the title printed on the spine. Box sizes follow the real cases, so a PS2 or GameCube game stands tall and thin, a PS1 jewel case is short, and a Game Boy box is small.

The boxes also show a few things about your copy. Jewel cases have the clear plastic hinge, cardboard boxes have a pale cut edge on top, a copy you've marked Sealed has a shrink-wrap shine, and if you ticked Obi band or Spine card for a copy, there's a paper band across its spine.

Hover over a box (or tap it once on a phone) to see the title, platform, region, edition and serial. Tap it again to open the game page.

Shelves hold up to 20 games on a phone and more on a wider screen, and re-arrange to fit. The filter box above the shelf works here too, and the shelf re-flows as you type.

## Ordering your shelf

Use the Order menu next to the filter to choose how your shelf is arranged:

- Console: grouped by platform (the default)
- Release date: oldest first, grouped by year
- Recently added: what you added last comes first, grouped by month
- Title: A to Z
- Series: sequels stand together (i.e. every Final Fantasy side by side, in release order). Tana works the series out from the title, so the odd one may land on its own.
- Region: Japanese editions, then North American, European and so on
- My own order: the order you arranged by hand (see below)

Each group stands apart with a small gap, like a bookend, and gets a little brass label on the shelf edge (Super Nintendo, 1996, Final Fantasy...). Your choice is saved to your account, so it's the same on every device, and it's also the order people see when they visit your public shelf.

## Arranging by hand

Tap Arrange, then drag any box to where you want it. On a phone, press on a box and slide it; the page scrolls by itself when you get near the top or bottom. On a computer you can also tab to a box and move it with the arrow keys (Home and End send it to either end).

The order saves as you go. The first time you move a box, the shelf switches to My own order, starting from whatever order it was in, so you don't have to start from scratch. If you want a fresh start, Start over from… lays the shelf out by console, release date, title, series, region or date added, and you can keep arranging from there. Tap Done when you're finished.

You can arrange a single platform too: pick it from the platform buttons, arrange, and the rest of your shelf stays where it was. New games you add go at the end of your own order.

At the top of your collection you'll see your numbers at a glance: games, platforms, how many are Japan-only, and how many are complete in box.

If you'd rather see a plain list, use the shelf and list buttons at the top right. The list follows the same order, with a heading for each group. Tana remembers your choice on each device.
