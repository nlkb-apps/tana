# Editing and removing copies

To change a copy, switch to the list view on your collection page. Each copy has its own buttons for Photos, Edit, and Remove.

Edit lets you change the condition and what's included. The price you paid, your value, the date you got it and your notes are under Price, date and notes; tap it to open those fields. If you've already filled any of them in, it opens by itself. Remove takes the copy off your shelf. This doesn't affect the game in the catalogue, only your own collection.

If you own several copies of the same edition, each one shows up as its own entry, so you can keep track of them separately (i.e. a sealed copy and a loose cartridge).
