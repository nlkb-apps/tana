# Privacy and your data

Here's what other people can see, and what stays with you.

- Public (if your collection is public): your shelf, your wantlist, your photos (unless hidden), your follower count, and your verified games
- Always private: your notes, prices paid, value estimates, and anything in a private collection

The full details are on the Terms, sources and credits page, linked at the bottom of every page.

You can delete your account at any time from Account with Delete my account.
