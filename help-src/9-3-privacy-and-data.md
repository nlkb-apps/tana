# Privacy and your data

Here's what other people can see, and what stays with you.

- Public (if your collection is public): your shelf, your wantlist, your photos (unless hidden), your follower count, and your verified games
- Always private: your notes, prices paid, value estimates, and anything in a private collection

Tana counts visits on its own database, without cookies. It records the page, the site that linked to it and a code made from your IP address and browser that changes every day and can't be turned back into either; your IP address itself isn't stored. It's only used to see how many people visit and where they come from.

The weekly letter only goes to members who said yes. Its "For you" lines are made from your own shelf and seen only by you. See [The weekly letter](6-13-weekly-letter.md).

The full details are on the Terms, sources and credits page, linked at the bottom of every page.

You can delete your account at any time from Account with Delete my account.
