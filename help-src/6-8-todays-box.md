# Today's Box · 今日の一箱

Today's Box is a daily game: guess a game from its box. There's one box a day for everyone, and a new one opens at midnight Japan time. Play at tana.gamingjapanese.com/box/, or tap Today's Box on the home page. You don't need an account to play.

## Playing

The box starts as an 8-bit mosaic, 8 blocks across, too coarse to read any title. You also see the platform. Type a game and pick it from the list. Each wrong guess, or each tap of Skip and sharpen, makes the box sharper and adds a clue: the year, then the publisher, the genre and the first letter of its Western title. You have six tries, and after the sixth you see the whole box.

Any version of the right game counts. If today's box is the Japanese Super Famicom release, picking the game by its English name is right too.

## The bonus round: どっちが日本版?

Once you've solved it, or used all six tries, you see two boxes of the same game: the Japanese one and a Western one. Both are cropped square and pixelated, so no text or box shape gives it away. Tap the one you think is Japan's. Then you see what changed between the two, from Tana's regional differences, or when and under what name the game came out in each region.

## Stamps, streaks and plates

Each day you play today's box earns a stamp on the month's stamp card, like a ラジオ体操 card. Days in a row make your streak. If you stamp every box in a month, that month's brass plate goes under your shelf for good, and visitors to your shelf see it too.

Boxes from earlier days can still be played from their link, but they don't earn stamps or count toward your streak.

## Without an account

You can play every day without an account. Your stamps and streak are kept on the device you play on. When you create a free account or sign in, today's and yesterday's results move into your account, and from then on your stamps, streak and plates follow you to any device.

## Sharing

When you finish, you get a result to post, like this:

- 棚 今日の一箱 No.12  3/6
- 🟥🟥🟩⬛⬛⬛
- 日本版 ✓  🔥5

It shows how many tries you needed and whether you got the bonus round, without giving the game away. Post on X opens a post with it ready, or tap Copy.

If you follow other collectors, their results for the day show under yours, again without the answer.
