# Your wantlist

Your wantlist is the list of games you're looking for. To add one, tap Want this on a release card on the game page. Tap it again to take it off.

Wants are kept per release, not per edition. So you can want the Japanese release of a game without wanting the North American one, but you can't (yet) want only a specific reprint.

You'll find everything you've marked under Wants & lists (Wants on a phone). The wantlist is the first card on the board there, and it's drawn as a shelf like your collection, in the same style as your main shelf. Use the Order menu to sort it by when you wanted it, console, release date, title, series or region, or switch to the list with the button at the top right, where each game has a Remove button.

A green tick on a box means you already own a copy of that release, which is handy when you want a second copy or a better one.

If your collection is public, your wantlist is public too. Visitors find it under the Wants & lists tab on your profile.
