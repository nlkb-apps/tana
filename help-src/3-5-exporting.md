# Exporting your collection

You can download your whole collection at any time from the collection page with Export as CSV.

The file opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, etc. and includes your games, platforms, regions, editions and serials, along with your own details like condition and notes. It's your data, so it's always yours to take with you.
