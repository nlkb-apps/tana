# How contributions are reviewed

Some contributions go live right away, and some are checked by a reviewer first.

- Live right away: new releases, new editions, and comments
- Reviewed first: corrections, regional differences, and photos

Reviewers look for a clear change and some kind of evidence. If something is rejected, you'll get a short note explaining why, and you're welcome to try again.

Approved corrections, differences and photos all count toward your contribution orb.
