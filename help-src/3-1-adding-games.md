# Adding a game to your collection

There are a few ways to put a game on your shelf, depending on where you are.

**From search.** When you're signed in, every game in the search results has a circle on the right. Tap it and the game goes straight onto your shelf, filled in. Tap it again to take it off. If the game came out on more than one platform, Tana asks which one you have.

The circle adds your usual edition, which is the Japanese one unless you change it in Account (see Account settings). If the game never came out in that region on that platform, Tana adds the edition that did, and the message at the bottom tells you which one went on.

**From a game page.** Tap Add to collection at the top of the page. If there's only one edition, it goes on right away. If there are several, a list of every edition comes up (region, label and serial) and you tap the one you have. You can also use the Add to collection button next to any single edition further down the page.

**Add games button.** On your collection page, Add games gives you every other way in one place: search, scan the barcode on the box, tick off a whole platform, or import a list.

After a game goes on your shelf, the message at the bottom has Add details and Undo. Add details opens your copy so you can fill in:

- Condition: Sealed, Mint, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Junk (ジャンク)
- What's included (box, manual, cartridge or disc, obi, etc.)
- Under Price, date and notes: the price you paid, your own estimate of its value, when you got it, and notes

Notes and prices are private. Nobody else can see them, even if your collection is public.

If you own more than one copy of the same edition, add each one separately (Add another copy on the game page). Each copy has its own condition and photos.
