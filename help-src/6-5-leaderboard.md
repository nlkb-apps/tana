# The leaderboard and verified games

The leaderboard ranks collectors by verified games. You'll find it under Community > Leaderboard, and you can filter it by platform.

A game counts as verified when it has an approved photo of that copy, uploaded by its owner. Owning a game isn't enough on its own, which keeps the board honest. If you own several copies of the same game, each copy needs its own photos to count.

Next to each collector you'll also see how many of their verified games are Japan only.

To climb the board, add photos of your own copies from your collection (see Adding photos of your copies). If your collection is private, you'll still see your own position, but nobody else will.
