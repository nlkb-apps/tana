# Hiding photos on your public shelf

If you'd like to keep your photos to yourself, you can hide them from your public shelf. Turn on Hide my photos on my public shelf in Account, or from the Share panel on your collection.

Your photos still count toward verification and the leaderboard. They just won't show up when other people look at your shelf.
