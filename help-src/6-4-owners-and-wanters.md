# Who owns and wants each edition

On every game page, each edition shows how many collectors own it, and each release shows how many collectors want it, i.e. "3 collectors own this edition" or "2 collectors want this release".

Public collectors are listed by name, so you can tap through to their shelf. Private collectors are counted but never named.
