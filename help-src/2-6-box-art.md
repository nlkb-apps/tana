# Box art

Most games on Tana show their box art: on the game page, next to each release, in search results and in My 9. More than three quarters of all releases have it, and almost every Famicom, Super Famicom, Mega Drive, PC Engine, Game Boy, N64, PlayStation, Saturn, Dreamcast, DS and Wii release.

## The region patch

Every box carries a small patch in the corner: JP, NA, EU and so on. The patch is always the region of the release you're looking at, not the region printed on the art.

When a release has no art of its own yet, Tana may show the box from another region of the same game on the same platform, so you still recognise it. A Japanese release showing the North American box still says JP. Hover over the box to see which release the art comes from.

## Where it comes from

- Box art scans collected by libretro-thumbnails, matched to Tana's releases by product code first and by title second
- The cover image from the game's English Wikipedia article, for newer games that have no scan yet
- Members' photos: an approved photo of the front of a copy becomes the box art for that release, unless the release already has art from its own region

Tana checks for new art every night, and the list of scans is refreshed every week.

## When there's no box art yet

Games with no art show a plain label: the platform's colour band, the title and the Japanese title. The quickest way to fill one in is to photograph the front of your copy and add it as a photo. Once an editor approves it, it becomes the box art.

## Wrong box art

If a box is for the wrong game or the wrong version, tap Wrong box art? under the release on the game page. A second report from another member takes it down, and so does a single report from an editor. Box art that has been taken down doesn't come back by itself.
