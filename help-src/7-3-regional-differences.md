# Documenting regional differences

Regional differences are the changes between versions of a game, i.e. censored content, different box art, a changed soundtrack, extra levels, or features that were cut. This is one of the things Tana cares about most.

To add one, open the game page and use the Regional differences section. You'll pick the other region and a category, write a short summary, describe the Japanese side and the other side, and add a source.

Bring evidence. A screenshot, a scan, a manual page, or a video timestamp all work.

Differences from members are sent for review before they're published. Approved ones count toward your contribution orb.
