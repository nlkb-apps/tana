The catalogue is built from these sources, with thanks:

- **MAME software lists**, CC0 — https://github.com/mamedev/mame/tree/master/hash
- **Wikidata**, CC0 — https://www.wikidata.org
- **Redump** disc data, via the **libretro database**, CC BY-SA 4.0 — http://redump.org, https://github.com/libretro/libretro-database

of Science and Technology (NAIST), used under the IPADIC licence.
