Regional differences

  • Censorship, North America
    Alcohol replaced with soft drinks, bars became cafés
    Japan
    酒場で「酒」を飲む。トマ「おやじ、酒！！」、アイテム「トマのさけ」、タバン「祭りで飲む酒はうめーぜ！」
    North America
    Cafés serve soda and cider. Toma orders cider, the item becomes "Toma's Pop", and Taban praises lemonade. The fair's drinking contest is a "soda guzzling" contest.
  • Text, North America
    魔王 (Maō, "Demon King") renamed Magus; his real name ジャキ became Janus
    Japan
    魔王（まおう）。本名はジャキ。
    North America
    Magus. Real name Janus.
  • Text, North America
    Condiment-named generals Vinegar, Mayonnaise and Soy Sauce became Ozzie, Flea and Slash
    Japan
    ビネガー・マヨネー・ソイソー（酢・マヨネーズ・醤油が由来の「三魔騎士」）
    North America
    Ozzie, Flea and Slash. Ozzie is named after Ozzy Osbourne.
  • Text, North America
    Frog speaks archaic, knightly English in the US version
    Japan
    カエル（グレン）は普通の、真面目でくだけた口調で話す。
    North America
    Frog speaks in a mock-Elizabethan style ("thou", "hast"), a characterisation added in localisation.
